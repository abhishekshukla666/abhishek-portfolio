## Summary

Pathya does not collect any personal data. Everything you tell the app, including your Prakriti,
diet, allergies and health details, stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else.

This policy applies to the Pathya app for iPhone, published by Abhishek Shukla. It will be updated
before an Android version is released.

## What Pathya keeps on your phone

Pathya stores a few things on your device so it can personalise its guidance. All of it is optional,
and none of it leaves your phone.

| What | Why Pathya keeps it |
|---|---|
| Your name, if you add one | To greet you |
| Your Prakriti quiz answers and result, or a dosha you pick yourself | To work out which foods suit your constitution |
| Whether you keep a Jain diet, follow Chaturmas, your month system and Ekadashi tradition | To show the foods, customs and dates that fit how you live |
| Food allergies and intolerances | To hide foods you can't eat |
| Health details you choose to add (pregnancy or breastfeeding, diabetes, high blood pressure, regular medication) | To be more careful: adjusting some foods' verdicts and holding back fasting plans |
| Reminder choices | To send meal, season and vrat reminders |
| Your language, and small app settings (whether you've seen the tour, which guide chapters you've read, when you accepted the terms) | To keep the app as you left it |

Pathya treats health details as sensitive. It uses them only to adjust what the app shows you, on
your phone.

**Location** is not stored. If you allow it, Pathya takes one reading while the app is open to work
out the calendar, and keeps it only until the app closes.

## What we don't do

- **No account.** You never sign up or log in, so we hold no name, email or phone number.
- **No server.** Pathya has no online service of its own. Its food guidance and calendar are built
  into the app.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Pathya contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools,
  and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- **No selling or sharing.** We have no data about you to sell or share, and will never use health
  information for advertising or marketing.

If we ever add a tool that sends data off your phone, we will update this policy first and tell you
in the app.

## Permissions

Pathya asks for two permissions, both optional. The app works without either.

| Permission | What it's for | If you say no |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Working out sunrise, tithi, Ritu and vrat timings for where you are. Used on your phone and never sent anywhere. | Pathya uses Ujjain's calendar instead |
| Notifications | Meal reminders, Ritu Sandhi alerts and vrat reminders, scheduled on your phone | No reminders; everything else works |

You can change either at any time in your phone's Settings.

## Backups and iCloud

Pathya never stores your health details (health conditions and allergies) in iCloud or any other
cloud service, and leaves them out of iPhone backups. If you move to a new phone or reinstall the
app, you add them again.

Your other settings, such as your name, diet choices and reminders, may be included in your phone's
own backup, which Apple holds under its privacy policy, not ours.

## Your choices

- **Change anything** at any time on the Me screen, including removing health details you added.
- **Delete everything** with "Delete my data" on the Me screen, or by uninstalling Pathya. Either
  removes it from your phone.
- **Ask us** anything about your data using the contact details below. Since we hold no data about
  you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children, purchases, changes and contact

**Children.** Pathya is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything
from them; it collects nothing from anyone.

**Purchases.** Pathya is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added, Apple will
handle the payment under its own privacy policy; we will receive only a confirmation that a purchase
was made, never your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

**Not medical advice.** Pathya shares traditional Ayurvedic knowledge. It is not a medical device and
does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Please consult your doctor before changing
your diet or fasting.

**This website.** These pages set no cookies and use no analytics. They are hosted on GitHub Pages
and load their fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address under their own privacy
policies.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app
before the change takes effect.

**Contact.** Abhishek Shukla, [apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com](mailto:apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com).
