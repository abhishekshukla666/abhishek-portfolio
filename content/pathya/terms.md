## 2. What Pathya is

Pathya shares what classical Ayurvedic texts and Indian food traditions say about food, each tip
labelled with where it comes from. It is for learning and reflection about everyday eating.

## 3. Not medical advice

doctor. Please consult a qualified doctor before fasting or changing your diet, especially if you

## 4. About the content

Pathya's content comes from classical texts, dharmic and folk traditions, and present-day readings
of them, and each tip names where it comes from. Traditions differ, and the content may contain
mistakes. What you eat is your own decision.
