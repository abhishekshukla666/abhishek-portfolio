Questions, a mistake in a verse, or an idea for Pathya? Write to
[apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com](mailto:apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com). Please mention your
phone model and the app version, shown at the bottom of the Me screen.

## Common questions

**The vrat dates or sunrise look wrong.**
Pathya works these out from where you are. If location is off, it uses Ujjain's calendar. Turn on
location for Pathya: on iPhone in Settings ▸ Pathya ▸ Location; on Android in Settings ▸ Apps ▸
Pathya ▸ Permissions ▸ Location.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that reminders are on in the Me screen, and that notifications are allowed: on iPhone
in Settings ▸ Pathya ▸ Notifications; on Android in Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Pathya ▸ Notifications.

**How do I retake the Prakriti quiz?**
Open the Me screen and start the quiz again from your Prakriti card.

**How do I delete my data?**
Use "Delete my data" at the bottom of the Me screen, or uninstall the app. Everything is on your
phone, so nothing is left anywhere else.

**I found a mistake in the content.**
Thank you. Write to us with the food or verse, and the source shown under it, and we'll check it
against the text.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Pathya is for learning about food in Ayurveda and Indian traditions. Please consult your doctor
before fasting or changing your diet.
