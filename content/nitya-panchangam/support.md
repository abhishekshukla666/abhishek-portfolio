[apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com](mailto:apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com). Please mention your

## Common questions

**The sunrise or tithi times look wrong.**
Nitya Panchangam works these out for the place it is using. If location is off, it uses Ujjain
(and says so). Allow location for the app in your phone's settings, or choose your city in
the app.

**An Ekadashi shows two dates.**
The Smarta and Vaishnava traditions sometimes keep an Ekadashi a day apart, and the app shows both.
Choose your tradition in Settings › Ekadashi tradition, and its day comes first, with its own Paran time.

**I'm not getting notifications.**
Check that the kind you want — morning panchang, festivals, tithi, muhurat, chaughariya, hora or
lagna — is on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's
settings.

**How do I add a widget or watch complication?**
Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add Nitya Panchangam from the widget
gallery. On Apple Watch, the complications come with the watch app: edit your watch face and choose
Nitya Panchangam.

**How do I change the language?**
Settings › Language: Hindi, English, Gujarati, Kannada, Telugu or Marathi.

**How do I turn off the bell?**
Settings › Sound: All sounds, Essential (the default) or Sound off.

**How do I delete my data?**
Everything is stored on your phone, so uninstalling the app removes it all. Nothing is kept anywhere
else.

**Is this a prediction app?**
No. Nitya Panchangam is traditional calendar reckoning. It does not make predictions, or give
medical, financial or legal advice.
