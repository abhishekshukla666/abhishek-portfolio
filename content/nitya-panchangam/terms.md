## 2. What Nitya Panchangam is

Nitya Panchangam calculates a Hindu calendar (panchang) — sunrise and sunset, tithi, nakshatra,
yoga, karana, muhurats, festivals and vrat days — on your device, for your location. It is
traditional calendar reckoning, for information and personal use.

## 3. Not advice or prediction

Nitya Panchangam does not make predictions, and nothing in it is medical, financial, legal or
other professional advice. For an important ceremony, please also consult your family priest or a
published panchang of your own tradition.

## 4. About the calculations

The calculations use the Swiss Ephemeris and established panchang rules. Regions and traditions
keep some days differently, and the app may contain mistakes. How you use its timings is your own
decision.
