## 2. What Nitya Kundli is

Nitya Kundli calculates a Vedic (sidereal) birth chart and its standard tables — divisional charts,
dashas, transits, strength and Guna Milan — on your device, from the birth details you enter. It is
a calculation tool for learning and personal use.

## 4. About the calculations

Positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris and standard Jyotish rules, with the ayanamsa you choose.
Schools of Jyotish differ, a chart is only as accurate as the birth details entered, and the app may
contain mistakes.
