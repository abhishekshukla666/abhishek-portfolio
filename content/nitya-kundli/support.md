Questions, a chart that looks wrong, or an idea for Nitya Kundli? Write to
[apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com](mailto:apple.abhishek.shuklaa@gmail.com). Please mention your
phone model and the app version, and, if a chart looks wrong, the birth details you entered.

## Common questions

**A chart doesn't match one I have from elsewhere.**
Check the birth time and place first — a few minutes can change the lagna. Then check the ayanamsa
in Settings: Nitya Kundli uses Lahiri by default, and other software may use another.

**I don't know the exact birth time.**
Choose “Time unknown?” when entering the details, for an approximate chart.

**How do I enter birth details quickly?**
Paste them from a WhatsApp message, in any of the six languages.

**How do I organise the family's kundlis?**
Give each one a relation — Self, Family, Friends or Clients — and a colour. You can change both from
Edit.

**Can I look at a chart without saving it?**
Yes. It stays under Recently Viewed for 24 hours, then disappears.

**How do I share a kundli or a Milan?**
Share it as a PDF from the chart, to anyone you choose.

**Can Nitya Panchangam mark good days for me?**
Turn on sharing in Nitya Kundli's Settings. Only each profile's name, rashi and nakshatra are shared,
on your phone.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete a kundli from your list, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Nothing is kept anywhere
else.
