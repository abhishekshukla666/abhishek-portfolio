## Summary

Nitya Kundli does not collect any personal data. Birth details you enter stay on your phone, and
every chart is calculated there. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and
no tracking.

This policy applies to the Nitya Kundli app for iPhone and Android, and its widgets, published by
Abhishek Shukla.

## What Nitya Kundli keeps on your phone

| What | Why it is kept |
|---|---|
| The kundlis you save: name, date, time and place of birth | To show their charts, dashas and Milan again |
| Each kundli's group (Self, Family, Friends, Clients) and colour | To organise your list |
| A kundli you view without saving | Kept under Recently Viewed for 24 hours, then removed |
| Your settings: language, theme, chart style, ayanamsa, notifications | To remember your choices |

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Location

Location is optional and used only while you use the app. It shows today's panchang and the hours
of a personal muhurat for where you are. It is never used for a birth place, which you type
yourself. If you decline, the app uses a default place and tells you so. Your location is not
stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you turn them on: a morning message, Gochar alerts, a
dasha-change alert and muhurat reminders. They are scheduled on your phone; no notification server
is involved.

## Sharing with Nitya Panchangam

This is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, each saved profile's name, rashi and
nakshatra are placed in a space on your phone that Nitya Panchangam can read, so it can mark good
days for you. Nothing leaves the device. Turn the setting off to stop sharing.

## PDFs you share

You can share a kundli or a Milan as a PDF. It is created on your phone and goes only where you
choose to send it.

## Purchases

There are no in-app purchases in the current version.

## Children

Nitya Kundli is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete a kundli from your list whenever you like. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has
stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, nothing remains with us.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
