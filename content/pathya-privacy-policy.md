**Effective:** November 1, 2026

## Summary

Pathya does not collect any personal data. Everything you tell the app, including your Prakriti, diet, allergies and health details, stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else.

This policy applies to the Pathya app for iOS and Android, published by Nitya. It takes effect on November 1, 2026.

## What Pathya keeps on your phone

Pathya stores a few things on your device so it can personalise its guidance. All of it is optional, and none of it leaves your phone.

| What | Why Pathya keeps it |
| --- | --- |
| Your Prakriti quiz answers and result | To work out which foods suit your constitution |
| Whether you keep a Jain diet, and whether you follow Chaturmas | To show only foods and customs that fit how you eat |
| Food allergies and intolerances | To hide foods you can't eat |
| Health details you choose to add (pregnancy, diabetes, high blood pressure, regular medication) | To be more careful: adding cautions to some foods and holding back fasting plans |
| How you feel today (mood and digestion), if you use it | To suggest a tip for the day |
| Your Panchang location, or a city you pick | To work out the Hindu calendar, Ritu and vrat timings for where you are |
| Reminder times | To send meal, season and vrat reminders |

Pathya treats health details as sensitive. It uses them only to adjust what the app shows you, on your phone.

## What we don't do

- **No account.** You never sign up or log in, so we hold no name, email or phone number.
- **No server.** Pathya has no online service of its own. Its food guidance and calendar are built into the app.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Pathya contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- **No selling or sharing.** We have no data about you to sell or share, and never will use health information for advertising or marketing.

If we ever add a tool that sends data off your phone, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app.

## Permissions

Pathya asks for two permissions, both optional. The app works without either.

| Permission | What it's for | If you say no |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Working out sunrise, tithi and vrat timings for where you are. Your location is used on your phone and never sent anywhere. | Pick your city by hand instead |
| Notifications | Meal reminders, alerts before each Ritu changes, and vrat reminders the evening before | No reminders; everything else works |

You can change either at any time in your phone's settings.

## Backups and iCloud

Pathya never stores your health details (health conditions, allergies and how you feel each day) in iCloud or any other cloud service, and leaves them out of iPhone and Android device backups. If you move to a new phone or reinstall the app, you add them again.

Your other settings, such as diet and reminder times, may be included in your phone's own backup, which Apple or Google hold under their privacy policies, not ours.

## Your choices

- **Change anything** at any time on the Me screen, including removing health details you added.
- **Delete everything** with "Delete my data" on the Me screen, or by uninstalling Pathya. Either removes all of it from your phone at once.
- **Ask us** anything about your data using the contact details below. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children, purchases, changes and contact

**Children.** Pathya is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them; it collects nothing from anyone.

**Purchases.** Pathya is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added later, Apple or Google will handle the payment under their own privacy policies; we would receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details, and this policy would be updated first.

**Not medical advice.** Pathya shares traditional Ayurvedic knowledge. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Please consult your doctor before changing your diet or fasting.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
